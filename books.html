Science Fiction & Fantasy

The Dispossessed

by Ursula K. Le Guin ·

A society is divided into two factions, one capitalist on the main planet and one anarchist on the moon. A physicist from the anarchist society travels to the capitalist planet to share his discoveries. From there, the contrast between the two factions is analyzed through his perspective.

The cold war analogy is pretty obvious, and if you watch/read science fiction, you'll find the same premise in multiple works. What stands out to me are the characters: from the protagonist to the minor ones, each has a full personality, in contrast to the one-dimensional people you can find in standard sci-fi. Also, there is a cameo by us, the humans, that is just wonderful.
